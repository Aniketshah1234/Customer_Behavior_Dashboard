<h2 id="exploratory-data-analysis-eda">📊 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)</h2>

### Key Insights:
- Majority of customers are **non-subscribers (~73%)**  
- Clothing category generates the highest revenue  
- Young adults contribute the most to sales  
- Customer ratings average around **3.75**  

### Patterns Observed:
- Strong relationship between product category and sales  
- Age group significantly impacts purchasing behavior  
- Subscription users show higher engagement  

---

<h2 id="research-questions--key-findings">❓ Research Questions & Key Findings</h2>

### Research Questions
1. What factors influence customer purchasing behavior?  
2. Which categories generate the most revenue?  
3. How does customer segmentation affect sales?  

### Key Findings
- Clothing is the **top-performing category**  
- Young adults are the **highest revenue contributors**  
- Subscription adoption is low (~27%)  
- Customer satisfaction (rating 3.75) can be improved
